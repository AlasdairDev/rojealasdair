# Security policy

## Scope

This is a static portfolio site. In scope: the HTML, CSS and JavaScript in
this repository, the deployed site, and its response headers.

## Reporting

Report suspected issues privately through LinkedIn:

https://www.linkedin.com/in/roje-alasdair-evangelista-00630b291/

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Include the URL, the
steps to reproduce it, and what you expected to happen.

## Out of scope

- Missing headers on hosts that ignore `_headers` (GitHub Pages does)
- Denial of service, rate limiting and spam
- Automated scanner output with no demonstrated impact
- The content or security of third-party sites linked from here

## What to expect

I am a student, not a company. I reply when I can, usually within a week.
There is no bounty. I will credit you if you want the credit.

## Safe harbour

If you test within scope, in good faith, and without touching data that is
not yours, I will not pursue action.
