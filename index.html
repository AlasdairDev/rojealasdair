FAQ

05 Plain answers

01What is it you actually do?

Offensive security — web apps and Android, self-taught, in labs or under written permission. I'm also finishing a BSIT at PUP Manila and running 777heaven Records, a fully independent label with 39 artists.

02Do you have certifications?

No — not yet. The repos, the write-ups and the threat model on D1zzy are the evidence I'd rather be judged on. A certificate is a hiring filter, not permission to test anything; permission comes from the person who owns the system.

03What does “authorised scopes only” mean?

I only test systems I own, systems built for practice, or systems where someone with authority has said yes in writing. Unauthorised access is a crime here and everywhere else.

04Can I send music to the label?

Yes — PH-based, non-mainstream. Use the demo form ↗. Links only; everything stays yours.

05Open to work or collaborations?

Yes — security work and label collaborations. LinkedIn ↗ is the fastest route; the demo form is the door for music.
