Cybersecurity student at PUP Manila — networks as the foundation, security as the goal — and I run 777heaven Records, a 100% independent label with 39 artists.

By hand — plain HTML/CSS/JS, no frameworks. The background is a WebGL/GLSL grain shader I wrote, with a soft light that follows your cursor.
