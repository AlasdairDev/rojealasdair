About

Web, Android and networks
Self-taught

I study BSIT at PUP Manila. Network engineering is the foundation. Cybersecurity is the goal, and right now I'm working through advanced network pentesting on my own.

Most of what I know came from breaking my own setups and rebuilding them. Class gave me the basics; docs, Wireshark and stubbornness did the rest.

I started 777heaven Records in 2024 so Manila's bedroom artists could keep their music. Forty-five artists and bands are on the roster now.

01What is it you actually do?

I test web apps and Android builds, mostly in a lab and always with permission. I'm learning advanced network pentesting, finishing a BSIT at PUP Manila, and running a 45-artist label.

02Do you have certifications?

Three: AI Essentials (UP Open University), Introduction to Cybersecurity (Cisco Networking Academy) and Fortinet Certified Fundamentals. Details are in About; the repos are still better evidence.

03What does “authorised scopes only” mean?

I test systems I own, practice labs, or something where the owner said yes in writing. No exceptions.

04Can I send music to the label?

Yes, if you're in the Philippines and don't fit the mold. Send links through the demo form ↗, not files. You keep your music.

05Open to work or collaborations?

Yes. Security work, internships, and label collabs. LinkedIn ↗ is fastest.

06What are you learning right now?

Advanced network pentesting. I build VLAN and routing labs in Packet Tracer and work through them on my own, with web and Android in the rotation.

07What tools do you use?

Nmap, Wireshark, Metasploit and Burp Suite. Linux, Python, Bash and MySQL. Packet Tracer for network labs.
