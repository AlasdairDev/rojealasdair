03

About

Web apps and Android
Self-taught

I study BSIT at PUP Manila. Networks came first, then web apps and Android.

Most of what I know came from breaking my own setups and rebuilding them until they worked. Class gave me the fundamentals. The rest came from docs, Wireshark captures, and not wanting to leave a half-finished project alone.

777heaven Records started in 2024. Manila has plenty of bedroom artists. It also has plenty of people happy to take a percentage. The label exists so the artists keep theirs. Forty-five artists and bands are on the roster now.

Certifications

No certificates yet. I test in a lab, on hardware I own, or with written permission, and I write down what I find. A cert helps with hiring filters. It doesn't tell you how someone works, so I'd rather show the repos and add the cert when it's done.

What I test

Authorised scopes only

a

Web applications

Login flows, session handling, access checks, and the input fields everyone forgets. I write findings up the way an engagement report expects.

b

Android

Static pass with jadx and apktool, then dynamic work with Frida and MobSF. Manifest, local storage, traffic, auth.

c

Networks and wireless

Nmap and Wireshark, plus WPA handshakes on the wireless side. I build VLAN and routing labs in Packet Tracer. There is no native build for Aurora DX, so I wrote down the workaround. n3rve came out of that.

FAQ

Five answers
Updated 2026

01What is it you actually do?

I test web apps and Android builds, mostly in a lab and always with permission. I'm finishing a BSIT at PUP Manila and running 777heaven Records, a 45-artist label I started in 2024.

02Do you have certifications?

No. Everything I know came from practice, docs, and breaking my own setups. A cert helps get past HR, but it doesn't tell you whether someone is careful. I'll get one. I'm not waiting on it.

03What does “authorised scopes only” mean?

I test systems I own, practice labs, or something where the owner said yes in writing. No exceptions.

04Can I send music to the label?

Yes, if you're in the Philippines and making something that doesn't fit the mold. Send links, not files, through the demo form ↗. You keep your music.

05Open to work or collaborations?

Yes. Security work, internships, and label collabs. LinkedIn ↗ is fastest.
