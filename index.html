FAQ

Plain answers
01What is it you actually do?

Cybersecurity student at PUP Manila — networks as the foundation, offensive security as the direction. I test web apps and Android builds, and I run 777heaven Records, a 100% independent label with 39 artists.

02How did you build this site?

By hand — plain HTML, CSS and JS, no frameworks. The background is a WebGL/GLSL shader I wrote: a scanline field with a soft light that follows your cursor.

03Can I send music to the label?

Yes — PH-based, non-mainstream. Use the demo form ↗. Links only; everything stays yours.

04What tools do you use?

Security: Nmap, Wireshark, Metasploit, Burp Suite. Systems: Linux, Python, Bash, MySQL. Web: plain HTML/CSS/JS and raw WebGL.

05Open to work or collaborations?

Yes — security opportunities and label collabs. Reach me on LinkedIn ↗, or the demo form for music.

06Do you have security certifications?

No — not yet. Everything I know is self-taught, tested in labs or with written permission. The repos and their security write-ups are the evidence I'd rather be judged on. A certificate is a hiring filter, not a licence to practise; it's the next box to tick, not the reason the work exists.
